Privacy Policy

This is the in-app, offline version. For the latest published version, visit https://farazsocialh.github.io/sportageobd/privacy.

1. About this policy

Sportage OBD ("we", "us", "our") is an OBD-II diagnostics and car-launcher app for Kia Sportage. This policy explains how the app handles information on your Android device.

Most vehicle data stays on your phone or head unit. When the app does send anything to us, it is anonymous and used only to keep the app stable.

2. Information we collect locally on your device

While the app is running, it keeps the following on your device and does not transmit it unless a diagnostic (crash) library does so on its own:

  • OBD-II readings: engine speed (RPM), coolant temperature, fuel level, throttle position, engine load, battery voltage, intake air temperature, instantaneous and average fuel economy, fuel used, and an estimate of current gear.
  • Diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), readiness-monitor status, and freeze-frame data, only when you choose to scan them.
  • Trip data: distance, duration, maximum speed, average speed, and fuel used, while the trip computer is active.
  • Bluetooth adapter information only as needed to connect to your ELM327-style adapter: the device name and the MAC address the adapter reports while pairing and connected. We do not use this for advertising or profiling, and we do not keep a list of adapters you have connected across reinstalls for that purpose.

The app does not record your voice, messages, contacts, or the contents of other apps. To scan for Bluetooth devices and to show the map, Android requires location permission; that is the only location use in the app, and you can deny it without breaking OBD diagnostics.

3. Diagnostic (crash) reports — optional

The app includes an optional crash-reporting library (Firebase Crashlytics). If you enable it, it may send an anonymous crash report to help us fix stability problems. The report may include:

  • A stack trace and the Android version, device model, and app version at the time of the crash.
  • Non-personal context such as the screen or feature being used.

It does not include your vehicle VIN, your name, your contact details, the contents of your trips, or the specific DTCs you scanned. You can turn crash reporting off from Settings. If you leave it on, reports are still optional and we do not combine them with any personal identity.

4. Analytics

Anonymous usage and performance analytics — for example, whether the app starts successfully and which features are used — may be sent through standard developer tools where you have not turned them off. These are used only to improve the app. We do not use them to build a profile about you, to target advertising, or to sell information about you.

5. Data we do not keep about you

We do not knowingly collect the following, and the app does not send it to us:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or account identifiers.
  • Your precise location over time. The app may query coarse or fine location only when required by Android to scan for Bluetooth or to display a map tile, and it does not store a movement history.
  • Your contacts, calendar, call log, or other app data.

6. Local storage

Trip logs, DTC scans, and CSV telemetry exports are stored on your device. You can export a CSV file from the app to your own storage or another app; that file is yours and is not sent anywhere by the app unless you share it yourself. Clearing in-app preferences resets onboarding and UI settings; clearing the app's storage in Android Settings removes these local files.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The in-app version always applies. If we make material changes, we will note the update date on this page.
