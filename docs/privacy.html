SportageOBD Intelligence — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.sportage.obd

In short: no account, no advertising, no data sale. Vehicle data is decoded on the device. Trips, scan history and CSV logs stay on the device unless you export them. Analytics and crash reporting are off until you opt in.

The app does not read contacts, SMS, call log, calendar, photos, camera, microphone or files outside its own storage.

2. Network connections

  1. Map tiles. The map pane fetches tiles over HTTPS; the tile server sees your IP address and the viewed area's coordinates, as with any online map.
  2. Optional diagnostics (opt-in). If enabled: Firebase Crashlytics (stack trace, app version, device model, Android version, random installation ID) and Firebase Analytics (coarse events such as onboarding completion, connection success/failure, scan completion, page views). No vehicle data, location, VIN or free text is included.
  3. Optional AI explanations (opt-in, off by default). If a build exposes the bring-your-own-key AI feature and you enable it, only the displayed trouble code and sensor snapshot are sent to the provider you configured. The key stays on the device.

4. Your choices

Turn analytics and crash reporting on or off under Settings → Privacy & diagnostics in the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes all locally stored data.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone by default.

6. Changes and contact

Material changes are published here with a new date. Questions or data requests: open an issue.
