SportageOBD Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

SportageOBD is an Android car launcher and OBD-II vehicle diagnostics application. The app does not operate any servers and does not send your data to the developer. All vehicle data, location data, trips, fault codes and settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded, sold or shared.

Data the app can access

Vehicle data (OBD-II)

The app connects over Bluetooth to an OBD-II adapter in your vehicle and reads diagnostic data (speed, RPM, temperatures, fuel data, fault codes). This data stays on your device. The app is read-only by default and never modifies your vehicle unless you explicitly confirm a fault-code clear.

Location

Device location is used on-device for the map, heading and trip distances. Online map tiles are fetched from OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address like any network request, but not your location history.

Bluetooth

Used to find and connect your OBD adapter and identify paired devices.

Installed apps

The launcher lists installed apps so you can launch them, and detects music/radio/navigation apps. Processed on the device, never transmitted.

Optional AI explanations

If you enable the AI feature and configure your own API endpoint and key, fault-code text you choose to explain is sent directly to that endpoint. If not configured, nothing is ever sent. The key is stored only on your device.

Optional weather

The home screen can fetch current weather from Open-Meteo; approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo as part of that request.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: find and connect the OBD adapter
  • Location: map position, heading, trip distance
  • Internet / network state: map tiles, optional weather and AI
  • Query installed packages: launcher app drawer and media app detection
  • Notifications + foreground service: keep the OBD connection alive

Storage and deletion

All data is in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it; trip and fuel history screens also offer in-app resets.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
